About

My website is a three page construct of a shop of bakery items and cocktails. The site is called Honey and Hibiscus. I love sweets and drinks, one of my favorite things is going to bakeries to try their products and i think it would be a really fun and cool thing to own and run a bakery. I believed that i could make a site that is vibrant and beautifully designed as if i own a bakery and that I'm creating my own website.

There will be 4 pages. The first will show the home page and a little bit about the company and what to expect from this website. The next page will be the Dessert page that will have the images, descriptions, ingredients and calories of the desserts. The next page is the drinks page which will also have the images, descriptions, ingredients and calories of the drinks. The last page will be a forms page that will let you request specific and build your own desserts or drinks.
